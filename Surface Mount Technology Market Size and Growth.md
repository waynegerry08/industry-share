***Challenges:***

Making an electrical device is challenging because several elements, particularly in SMT, must be considered. High-frequency applications, thermal management, and stress on the PCB from an incorrect coefficient of thermal expansion (CTE) must all be considered while designing the device. The CTE makes it easier to comprehend how a change in temperature would affect an object's size. Additional elements, such as the impedance of the package signal lines, cross-talk noise between these lines, and inductance interconnects on the PCB, must be considered when designing for high-frequency applications, such as telephony and consumer electronics.
